What happens with your meeting

Three facts about this Mac, not a policy promise.

What leaves this Mac
Nothing. Recording, transcription, and notes run and stay here. Yawn has no account and makes no network calls with meeting data.
Where transcription runs
On this Mac, using the local speech model shown below.
What is kept, and for how long
Transcript and notes: until you delete them (deleted meetings stay in Trash for 30 days). Recording audio: 1, 7, or 30 days — you choose at the start of each meeting.

Speech model

Choose the model Yawn uses for transcripts. Meeting audio stays on this Mac.

Note model

An optional model that turns finished transcripts into meeting notes, on this Mac.
