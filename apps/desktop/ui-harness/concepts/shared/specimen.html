Focus and keyboard

Focus ring: 2px accent, 1px offset, on every control and row (:focus-visible). Tab order: toolbar left to right, sidebar rows top to bottom, document, inspector.

⌘R Record · ⌘. Stop · ⌘⇧P Pause · ⌘⇧S Sidebar · ⌘F Search · ⌘, Settings · ↑↓ move selection · ⏎ open · ⌫ Move to Trash · ⌘⌥T Open full transcript · Esc close inspector or sheet

Accessibility states

Large text: every size scales with the system setting; rows grow, nothing truncates that did not already, the measure holds in characters not pixels.

Increased contrast (?contrast=more, or the system setting): separators and secondary labels darken; no new colors. Reduced motion: every transition is 0 ms. Reduce transparency: the sheet dim is opaque panel.

VoiceOver: rows are a list of buttons named "title, status, date"; the live Record control announces elapsed time on demand, not continuously; the open claim is a disclosure whose expanded content is the cited turn.
