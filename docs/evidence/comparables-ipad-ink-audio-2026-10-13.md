# Comparables — handwriting with audio, from Mobbin, 2026-10-13

First comparables pass for the iPad in-person mode (roadmap step 3; brief
section "iPad in-person mode — decided 2026-10-13"). Source: Mobbin's iOS
library, searched by a delegated research agent (16 calls) and spot-checked by
the orchestrating session (3 calls). No frames are committed; links go to
Mobbin. Nothing here is a feature recommendation; the brief's rules still hold.

**What this cannot show.** Every layout is iPhone, not iPad. Stills cannot show
replay, highlighting, or gestures, so no frame shows ink replayed or a stroke
tapped to seek. Goodnotes' audio screens are on a blank page. Notability, Nebo
and Noteshelf returned nothing. The next comparables step is device captures of
Goodnotes or Notability running on the iPad.

**Seen directly by the orchestrator:** the Goodnotes playback sheet covering the
page, the Granola recording bar, and ElevenReader highlighting the passage being
read. The rest is the research agent's reading of the frames.

## How apps tie notes to audio

- Goodnotes keeps audio in a bottom sheet with Summary and Transcript tabs,
  scrubber, skip and speed. The sheet covers the lower half of the page
  ([sheet over page](https://mobbin.com/screens/fbc76ae9-cfb6-47b8-9921-3afd565bd67e),
  [sheet](https://mobbin.com/screens/213b91ad-7135-451c-9186-ae9113ecf937)).
  "Notes Playback: Spotlight" appears only as a setting label.
- Apple Notes puts the recording in the note as an inline card with a
  transcript preview ([playing](https://mobbin.com/screens/3f36d706-f82f-4d42-b10a-c995e4001a5e),
  [live transcript](https://mobbin.com/screens/903ddeb1-ec14-45a0-8283-6c3748b52b91)).
- Otter and Fabric are transcript-first: timestamped paragraphs over a scrubber
  ([Otter](https://mobbin.com/screens/26c472e8-30e4-4b2f-a413-c5e19adfe550),
  [Fabric](https://mobbin.com/screens/4be754a2-e4cb-4927-b04e-872b86a2bd07)).
- ElevenReader highlights the paragraph and word being read
  ([seen](https://mobbin.com/screens/fe788595-2deb-469c-b9ce-6cb0d0b4c6d6)).
  The agent also cites a "Back to current" pill after scrolling away
  ([cited, not seen by the orchestrator](https://mobbin.com/screens/5c627524-cd63-4845-885a-77c7dace046c)).

## Drawing versus scrolling

No frame explains finger versus Pencil or shows a Pencil-only switch. Apps
separate drawing from scrolling with a mode: Apple Notes enters and leaves
drawing with a check button ([frame](https://mobbin.com/screens/59cfcfbf-756d-4af0-ba2a-a92f1acccd43));
Craft puts a pan tool first ([frame](https://mobbin.com/screens/6011086c-42ca-43e5-aa73-2d1d6de04934));
Journal keeps ink in a bounded box ([frame](https://mobbin.com/screens/8f322825-d45a-4e43-aec1-9312fc419fd3)).
Yawn's finger-first rule (one finger writes, two scroll) has no captured
precedent here, so it needs its own explanation on the page.

## Recording, consent and keep period

- Granola: pause, timer, level meter and End at the foot of the note
  ([seen](https://mobbin.com/screens/a646dca1-d8b7-44b1-9e50-ed7c0db8a591)),
  plus a "Taking notes… 1:39 / End" banner outside the app
  ([seen](https://mobbin.com/screens/68a8fed7-ea39-426f-8353-f114d87780dd)).
- No meeting recorder frame shows a consent step or a keep-period choice.
  Nearest analogs: Telegram's notice that others see the chat is being recorded
  ([frame](https://mobbin.com/screens/7a4f6c3c-7a71-4a3f-a92c-759f70e99061)),
  and WhatsApp's 24 hours / 7 days / 90 days list with one explanatory
  paragraph ([frame](https://mobbin.com/screens/4109ac74-59e5-44c2-9add-e44b12738c57)).

## List of past meetings

- Otter: a card per conversation with title, time and duration, and a short
  summary, grouped by day ([frame](https://mobbin.com/screens/f314557f-6003-4d02-9b2c-d324e3a0d139)).
- Granola: plain rows with title and time under day headings
  ([frame](https://mobbin.com/screens/978cd9f4-a931-4b41-8088-cbb7a5b2bf22)).
- Goodnotes: a thumbnail grid with relative dates and no audio marker
  ([frame](https://mobbin.com/screens/f9dce871-ecc2-4753-9727-ec2c7b7d76eb)).

## Worth borrowing, worth avoiding

- Borrow Granola's recording bar: pause, timer, level and End at the foot of
  the page, with an outside-the-app banner while recording.
- Borrow the follow-along highlight (ElevenReader) for the transcript during
  replay, with a way back to the playing moment.
- Borrow a short keep-period list with one plain paragraph (WhatsApp).
- Avoid a sheet that covers the strokes the audio belongs to (Goodnotes).
- Avoid silent audio copies: Goodnotes' "Generate a new note" makes a copy of
  the recording ([dialog](https://mobbin.com/screens/a1296fae-28d3-432c-ba30-68a8ef59cfe4)),
  which would break a 1, 7 or 30 day keep promise.
